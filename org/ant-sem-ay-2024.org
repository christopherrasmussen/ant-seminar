*** Abstracts

***** /Heavenly elliptic curves and connections to complex multiplication/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2024-09-06
:END:
      [[https://crasmussen.faculty.wesleyan.edu/][Christopher Rasmussen]], Wesleyan University

We report on recent joint work with Cam McLeman (Michigan-Flint) on
finiteness results around heavenly elliptic curves over number fields,
and show that for almost all primes \(\ell\), there are only finitely
many quadratic number fields over which an elliptic curve exists which
is heavenly at \(\ell\). Generalizations of this result will also be
proved. We observe similar behavior between heavenly curves and elliptic
curves with complex multiplication, especially in terms of the trace of
Frobenius. We explain a calculation that determines all elliptic curves
defined over a quadratic number field which are both heavenly at some
prime \(\ell\) and also possess (potential) complex multiplication.

***** /Arboreal Galois groups with colliding critical points/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2024-09-20
:END:
      [[https://rlbenedetto.people.amherst.edu/][Robert Benedetto]], Amherst College

Let \(f\) be a rational function of degree \(d\) at least 2, defined
over a field \(K\) (usually \(\mathbb{Q}\)), and let \(x_0\) be in
\(K\). The backward orbit of \(x_0\), which is the union of the iterated
preimages \(f^{-n}(x_0)\), has the natural structure of a \(d\)-ary
rooted tree. Thus, the Galois groups of the fields generated by roots of
the equations \(f^n(z)=x_0\) are known as arboreal Galois groups. In
2013, Pink observed that when \(d = 2\) and the two critical points
\(c_1, c_2\) of \(f\) collide, meaning that \(f^m(c_1) = f^m(c_2)\) for
some \(m\) at least 1, then the arboreal Galois groups are strictly
smaller than the full automorphism group of the tree. We study these
arboreal Galois groups when \(K\) is a number field and \(f\) is either
a quadratic rational function (as in Pink's setting over function
fields) or a cubic polynomial with colliding critical points. We
describe the maximum possible Galois groups in these cases, and we
present sufficient conditions for these maximum groups to be attained.

***** /Local data of elliptic curves under quadratic twist/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2024-10-18
:END:
      [[https://manamiroy.github.io/][Manami Roy]], Lafayette College

Let \(K\) be the field of fractions of a complete discrete valuation
ring \(R\) with a perfect residue field of characteristic \(p\) and
\(E\) be an elliptic curve over \(K\). Tate’s Algorithm provides
instructions for computing the proper regular minimal model of \(E\)
over \(K\). As a result, we obtain the following local data for \(E\):
the Kodaira-Néron type, the conductor exponent, and the local Tamagawa
number. We will discuss how the local data of \(E\) change under
quadratic twists. In particular, for an odd characteristic, the change
of local data of \(E\) under twists is discussed for any \(K\). For
characteristic 2, we will discuss our results for \(\mathbb{Q}_2\).
Consequently, our study of elliptic curves over local fields answers how
the local data at a prime \(p\) and global Tamagawa number of rational
elliptic curves change under quadratic twists.

***** /Representations of affine quantum groups and equivariant homology of affine Grassmannians/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2025-03-28
:END:
      [[https://sites.google.com/view/eliecasbi/home][Elie Casbi]], Northeastern University

Let \(G\) be a semisimple complex Lie group and \(N\) a maximal
unipotent subgroup of \(G\). In their study of the equivariant homology
of the affine Grassmannian \(\mathrm{Gr}_{G^\vee}\),
Baumann-Kamnitzer-Knutson introduced an algebraic morphism
\(\overline{D}\) on the coordinate ring \(\mathbb{C}[N]\) providing a
powerful tool to compare distinguished bases of this algebra, such as
the Mirković-Vilonen basis arising from the geometric Satake
correspondence. In this talk we will focus on the simply-laced case and
present an alternative description of \(\overline{D}\) proposed in a
joint work with Jian-Rong Li, that relies on Hernandez-Leclerc’s
categorification of the cluster structure of \(\mathbb{C}[N]\) via
finite-dimensional representations of affine quantum groups. We will
then present our second work recently posted on the arXiv (also joint
with Jian-Rong Li) where we establish a large family of non-trivial
rational identities obtained by applying our construction to
Frenkel-Reshetikhin’s \(q\)-characters. If time allows, we will discuss
possible interpretations of these identities in terms of equivariant
homology, raising the question of natural geometric models associated to
representations of affine quantum groups.

***** /Supersingular isogeny-based cryptography/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2025-04-04
:END:
      [[https://www.uvm.edu/~cvincen1/][Christelle Vincent]], University of Vermont

Supersingular elliptic curves have been a subject of interest to
cryptographers since the early 2000s, when Charles, Goren and Lauter
proposed a hash function whose security assumption rests on the
difficulty of navigating the supersingular \(\ell\)-isogeny graph, for
\(\ell\) a prime different from the characteristic of the base field.
Subsequent investigation in turn tied the difficulty of navigating this
graph to the difficulty of computing the endomorphism ring of a
supersingular elliptic curve given its \(j\)-invariant. One line of
investigation towards understanding this object is to study the
endomorphisms of small degree (the "short vectors" in the lattice formed
by the endomorphism ring) and the "angles" between them.

In this talk we introduce post-quantum cryptography and more
specifically isogeny-based cryptography. We then present results on the
short vectors contained in the Gross lattice of the endomorphism ring of
a supersingular elliptic curve, and in particular what they reveal on
the field of definition of the underlying elliptic curve. This is joint
work with Chenfeng He, Gaurish Korpal and Ha Tran.

***** /A search bound for equivalence of integral Hermitian forms over a number field/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2025-04-11
:END:
      [[https://sites.google.com/wesleyan.edu/jacob-tolman/home][Jacob Tolman]], Wesleyan University

Given Hermitian matrices \(A\) and \(B\) with entries in the ring of
integers of a number field, one may ask whether or not they are
equivalent. Does there exist an integral change of basis, called a
transition matrix, that takes \(A\) to \(B\)? If so, is there an
algorithm to effectively produce it? By studying the actions of certain
related Lie groups and applying tools from homogeneous dynamics, we show
that if \(A\) and \(B\) are equivalent, then a transition matrix can be
found after a finite search.

***** /Abelian extensions arising from elliptic curves with complex multiplication/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2025-04-18
:END:
      [[https://asiminah.github.io/][Asimina Hamakiotes]], University of Connecticut

Let \(K\) be an imaginary quadratic field, and let \(\mathcal{O}_{K,f}\)
be an order in \(K\) of conductor \(f \geq 1\). Let \(E\) be an elliptic
curve with complex multiplication by \(\mathcal{O}_{K,f}\), such that
\(E\) is defined by a model over \(\mathbb{Q}(j(E))\), where \(j(E)\) is
the \(j\)-invariant of \(E\). Let \(N \geq 2\) be an integer. The
extension \(\mathbb{Q}(j(E), E[N])/\mathbb{Q}(j(E))\) is usually not
abelian; it is only abelian for \(N=2,3\), and \(4\). Let \(p\) be a
prime and let \(n \geq 1\) be an integer. In this talk, we will classify
the maximal abelian extension contained in
\(\mathbb{Q}(E[p^n])/\mathbb{Q}\).

***** /Models of CM elliptic curves with prescribed \(p\)-adic Galois image/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2025-05-02
:END:
      [[https://benjamin-york.github.io/][Benjamin York]], University of Connecticut

In 2015, Zywina classified all possible images of mod-\(p\) Galois
representations attached to elliptic curves defined over \(\mathbb{Q}\)
with complex multiplication (CM). In addition, this work also classified
which Weierstrass models of elliptic curves over \(\mathbb{Q}\) with
complex multiplication (CM) realized each possible image. In 2018,
Lozano-Robledo classified all possible images of \(p\)-adic Galois
representations attached to elliptic curves with complex multiplication
(CM). In this talk, we will discuss a classification of Weierstrass
models for CM elliptic curves with specified \(p\)-adic Galois image,
and discuss our methods for proving this classification. This is joint
work with Enrique González-Jiménez and Álvaro Lozano-Robledo.
