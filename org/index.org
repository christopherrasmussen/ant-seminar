*** Abstracts

***** /From Gauss composition to binary orthogonal modular forms/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2026-10-09
:END:
      [[https://haochenwumath.github.io][Haochen Wu]], Trinity College
      
Gauss composition identifies equivalence classes of binary quadratic
forms with ideal classes in quadratic orders. We revisit it over
Dedekind domains, showing that it is given by the Clifford and norm
maps, building on work of Kneser and Wood. We clarify the role of
orientations and the natural emergence of narrow class groups. As an
application, we show that binary orthogonal eigenforms correspond to
Hecke characters. Using theta series, we show the explicit connection
between Hilbert modular forms and orthogonal modular forms arising
from positive definite binary lattices over the ring of integers of a
totally real number field. This is joint work with John Voight.

***** /Heavenly elliptic curves over cubic fields/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2026-10-16
:END:
      [[https://sites.google.com/view/suzanneohara][Suzanne O'Hara]], Wesleyan University

The study of heavenly elliptic curves is motivated by a question of
Ihara which compares Galois extensions generated by arithmetic and
geometric methods. Knowing an elliptic curve \(E/K\) is heavenly an odd
prime \(\ell\) lets us deduce extra information about the representation of
the absolute Galois group, \(G_K\), on the \(\ell\)-torsion of the curve. Using
this, we deduce a formula for the mod \(\ell\) trace of \(E\) at primes \(p\)
distinct from \(\ell\). Previous work of Rasmussen and Tamagawa has shown
that for a fixed number field \(K\) of degree at most \(3\), there are on
finitely many \(K\)-isomorphism classes of heavenly elliptic curves when
we let \(\ell\) vary through all primes. Recently, McLeman and Rasmussen have
studied the set of heavenly elliptic curves where both the quadratic
field of definition and the prime \(\ell\) are allowed to vary. In this talk,
we discuss analogues of their results to the cubic field case and show
a further classification of heavenly elliptic curves which have a
“CM-like” trace.
