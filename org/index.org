*** Abstracts

***** /From Gauss composition to binary orthogonal modular forms/
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: 2026-10-09
:END:
      [[https://haochenwumath.github.io][Haochen Wu]], Trinity College
      
Gauss composition identifies equivalence classes of binary quadratic
forms with ideal classes in quadratic orders. We revisit it over
Dedekind domains, showing that it is given by the Clifford and norm
maps, building on work of Kneser and Wood. We clarify the role of
orientations and the natural emergence of narrow class groups. As an
application, we show that binary orthogonal eigenforms correspond to
Hecke characters. Using theta series, we show the explicit connection
between Hilbert modular forms and orthogonal modular forms arising
from positive definite binary lattices over the ring of integers of a
totally real number field. This is joint work with John Voight.
